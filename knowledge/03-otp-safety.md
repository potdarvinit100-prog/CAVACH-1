# OTP, PIN and credential safety

Source: CERT-In / bank fraud guidance (curated summary)
Applies to: OTP requests, KYC update messages, account-block threats

No legitimate bank, police officer or government official needs your OTP,
PIN, password or CVV to help you. Messages that threaten account closure
unless you update KYC immediately and share an OTP are a classic phishing
pattern. Go to the official website or app yourself — never through the
link in the suspicious message — and check the account status there.
A postal PIN code for delivery is not the same as a bank PIN; context
matters, which is why credential patterns in this product are phrases,
not bare words.
