# UPI fraud patterns

Source: RBI / NPCI public guidance summaries (curated)
Applies to: UPI collect requests, QR codes, refund and relative-impersonation scams

Common UPI patterns: a stranger impersonating a relative in urgent need,
a fake customer-care number found by searching online, a QR code or
collect request that actually takes money instead of receiving it, and a
refund offer that requires a small advance payment first. Verify a
relative by calling a number you already have, not by replying in the
same chat. Verify a business by its official website or app, not by a
number in a message. If a payment was sent to a fraudster, report on
1930 and cybercrime.gov.in and inform your bank immediately.
