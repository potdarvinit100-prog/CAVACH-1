# Verifying an authority call (digital arrest pattern)

Source: Maharashtra Cyber awareness guidance + CERT-In verification guidance (curated summary)
Applies to: police, court, tax or bank impersonation by call or video call

A genuine authority does not demand payment over a video call, does not ask
you to keep a case secret, and does not show a court document on screen as
proof of identity. A uniform, an emblem backdrop and a letterhead image can
all be staged. The safe verification step is: hang up, then call back only
on a number you already trust — never the number shown on screen. Use a
pre-agreed family safe word for relatives. Never share an OTP, PIN or
password with a caller, and never transfer money while under pressure.
