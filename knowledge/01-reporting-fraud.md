# Reporting financial cyber fraud in India

Source: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal guidance (curated summary)
Applies to: financial fraud, UPI fraud, phishing resulting in money loss

If money was lost, act in this order: stop further payments, call the
national cybercrime helpline 1930, report at cybercrime.gov.in, and inform
your bank through its official number. Keep the complaint reference and the
portal acknowledgement number. Recovery is not guaranteed; anyone who offers
recovery for an upfront fee should be treated as suspicious. Preserve the
message, link, phone number, UPI id, transaction reference and screenshots
before deleting anything.
